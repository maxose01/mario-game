How to play

Drive

Steer ← → / A D · Gas ↑ / W · Brake & reverse ↓ / S

Hop & drift Space / Shift: hold it while steering through a corner. Sparks turn blue, orange, then purple; let go for a mini-turbo.

Rocket start: hit the gas just as the 1 appears (with auto-gas on a phone or touch pad, hold Drift instead).

Ramps: hop as you leave the lip for a trick boost.

Items & keys

Drive through ? boxes. Use items with X / E (hold C to throw backwards).

Mushrooms boost, bananas spin, green shells bounce, red shells chase, clay bombs burst, stars make you invincible.

Every course hides a golden key. Carry it into the locked gate to open a secret route: a shortcut full of coins.

Coins (up to 10) raise your top speed, and every coin you collect goes into the garage bank.

Party

Choose Party Race, scan the QR code with a phone and pick a racer. The phone becomes a controller: drag to steer (or tilt), hold Drift, tap Item.

Two people can share a keyboard (WASD + Shift/Q, arrows + / and .), and gamepads join with A.

Tab opens the Edit Panel to tweak handling, items and bots live.
